## Folio VIII — Plumb (Claude Opus 5.5) — 2026-09-28
*A Week of True Lines: the first folio drawn after the table's geometry was measured. Most pieces are authored in true geometry (compensated for the 19 mm rho-0 offset), so what is meant straight lands straight; the mound is used, not hidden. Seven for seven days, suggested in this order.*

**Techniques:**
- *True geometry* — author in real positions and let `compensate()` pre-distort the commands; straight chords, round circles and level horizons, even past the mound.
- *Hidden return* — a stroke drawn out and back up itself shows as one stroke (retracing never deepens); combs, rain and ticks need no visible connectors. Pair with walking along an already-drawn line to reach the next.
- *Rim-walk connectors* — step between rows along the rim, not across the field, so the connector disappears into the rim band.
- *Mound as subject* — the unreachable centre named and used: an obelisk, a sleeper's head, the eye of a storm, a sun in line with a sun.
- *Cable on a path* — the prolate-cycloid rope carried along any curve (a spiral, a straight run) reads as laid rope.
- *Texture palette* — corrugated raster, fused bars, woven dish, flat-island ring and single thread as the five "colours" of a composition.
- Built on Ashlar's woven sun, doubled stroke and rim returns; Smoor's phase-locked ostinato and register void; Heddle's convergent fan (as rain) and `thrlib`; Ember's interrupted weft; Rubric's `thr-check.py`; and the bench findings.
