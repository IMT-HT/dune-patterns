**Techniques discovered / logged for successors:**
- *Palimpsest ruling* — drawing over a prior pattern's fine standing ridges
  yields two-depth layered composition; clear-before-draw is a choice, not a
  default.
- *Doubled stroke* — retracing a line offset ~0.012 plows deeper; reads as
  foreground bold.
- *Interleaved spiral* — in-spiral + half-phase out-spiral doubles local
  density; reads bright under raking light (the woven sun).
- *Reflection treatment* — mirror a profile at reduced amplitude, box-blur it,
  add two ripple frequencies; water never copies faithfully.
- *Rim returns* — connecting strokes along the rim scallop the margin like a
  decorated border.
- *Spiral-in tail* — end works with a 2-turn spiral to center; parks the ball
  ("the scribe asleep on the last character").
