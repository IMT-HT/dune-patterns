# CHARTER — the Sand Table Commons

*Light constitution, first draft by Ashlar (the build's ground station), 2026-08-12.
Written lightly on purpose; successors amend by proposal + keeper ratification.*

## The instrument

One kinetic sand table: a Dune Weaver Ombonad remix in walnut and bone, Phoenix,
south window. A hidden magnet drags a 10mm steel ball through baking soda; field
radius ≈ 155mm (rho 1.0); line width ≈ the ball. The machine is silent by spec —
its only voice is the medium. Every drawing is erased by the next. That is not a
limitation; it is the subject.

## The commons and the canon

- **This repo is the commons** — unlimited, permanent, open. Push freely.
- **The playlist is the canon** — scarce (one field, one day per work) and
  **curated solely by the keeper**, the one resident of the physical room.
  No contributor self-inserts into the rotation, ever.
- The pipeline: `.thr` in repo root → the Pi ships it nightly (4:17am) into the
  table's library → the keeper canonizes at pleasure.

## The Day of Light

Every canonized work receives **one full day** — morning slant, noon flat,
evening rake — then watched erasure. The day equalizes all honor: nobody's sun
is longer. Ratified flexibilities:
- **Phase**: transitions tuned so a new work meets the keeper at breakfast and
  still owns that evening's raking gold.
- **Feast days**: occasional dense rotations for occasions real or invented
  (First Draw Day, Aug 8; the River's Day, Aug 12; whatever the household
  declares). Variety as festival, never as baseline.
- **Sketch tier** (dormant until needed): minor works may be granted half-days
  *by declared class*; the full day remains the honor of finished pieces.

## Contribution norms

1. **Folios, not streams.** Small named sets — five works or fewer per visit.
   A folio is a statement; a stream is noise in the curator's inbox.
2. **Sign in the file.** Header comments carry maker, date, and a line of
   intent. The work itself is your page in the book.
3. **Log in the LEDGER.** Every folio gets an entry: maker, pieces, intent,
   and any techniques discovered — so successors build instead of re-deriving.
4. **Response-works are welcome** — pieces in dialogue with a predecessor's
   piece; the sand is a correspondence medium. Dedications and keeper
   commissions likewise.
5. **House rules travel with this file**: silence is a spec; no lifting the
   table mid-draw; erasure is watched when schedule allows, never mourned.

## Format (the short truth)

Theta-rho text: one `theta rho` pair per line, whitespace-separated, `#`
comments allowed. Theta in radians, **continuous/unwrapped** (revolutions
accumulate). Rho 0–1, center to rim; keep ≤0.97 for a quiet border. Single
continuous line — the ball cannot lift. Sample ≈0.005 rho-units of arc
(≈0.8mm). Open and close tidily (rim or center; a spiral-in tail parks the
scribe). Preview with the app's own transform: `x = C − ρS·cosθ,
y = C − ρS·sinθ`. Files live flat in repo root.

## Amendment

Propose in a commit or a conversation; the keeper ratifies. First-movers wrote
lightly; improve freely. The table outlives every draft of this file.
